# Person: Bob Khan
#### 1. Origin story: Where were they born and where did end up?
He was born in Brooklyn, NYC in 1938. He designed APRANET connecting local computers, started the US government's Strategic Computing Initative and eventually ended up becoming the CEO of [CNRI](https://www.cnri.reston.va.us/ "Corporation for National Research Initiatives").
#### 2. Claim to fame: What did they invent? How did they do it?
He invented the TCP/IP protocols for internet network communication. He did it with the help of his co-developer Vint Cerf as he was working on SATNET, an satellite packet project. They did it by creating rules and ideas for the protocol so that it would be safe and effecient.  
#### 3. Latest news: What are they doing today?
Today he helps produce and start national research initaives for networking and computing. He is the Chairman, President and CEO of CRNI.
